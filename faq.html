Visiting & Pricing

Do I need an appointment to visit?

Yes — we're open daily, by appointment only. Call us at 732-221-1925 or request a quote through the site and we'll set up a time that works for you.

How do I get pricing on materials?

Use the "Request a quote" button on any page, or give us a call. Most quote requests get a response with real pricing the same business day.

Are the prices shown on the website final?

The prices and stock levels you see are pulled live from our warehouse inventory, but they're estimates until confirmed directly by us. We'll always confirm final pricing when we follow up on your quote.

Can I pick up materials the same day I request a quote?

It depends on what's in stock and how our schedule looks that day. The best way to know for sure is to request a quote or call ahead — we'll let you know what's realistic for your order.

Materials & Stock

What materials do you carry?

Six core categories: flooring, doors, windows, vanities, shingles, and toilets — all stocked in our own warehouse, not special-ordered.

Do you carry a specific brand I'm looking for?

Brand availability varies by category and changes as we restock. The fastest way to check is to search for it directly on the site, or just give us a call.

Can you source something that's not listed on the site?

Reach out and let us know what you need — request a quote or call us, and we'll tell you what's possible.

Trade Accounts

Do you offer accounts for contractors and builders?

Yes — trade accounts get you volume pricing, net payment terms, and priority will-call pickup. Details and an application are on our About page.

How long does it take to get approved?

Applications are reviewed individually. We'll follow up directly once you apply to let you know next steps.
